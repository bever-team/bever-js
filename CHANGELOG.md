# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first public release.

- `parseBeverModel` and `createBeverApp` for semantic model 0.10, with validation.
- Presentation resolution with readable default labels.
- `createBeverClient` for records, lookups, account pickers, sign-in, and role assignment, with antiforgery support and `BeverHttpError`.
